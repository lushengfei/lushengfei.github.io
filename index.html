About

I'm an undergraduate in the Financial Economics Specialist program at the University of Toronto. My interests are in macroeconomics and international finance, and I'm currently working on empirical research using firm-level panel data.

I'm interested in graduate study in economics and in research roles that sit between academia and policy or industry.
